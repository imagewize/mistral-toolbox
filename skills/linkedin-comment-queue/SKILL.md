# LinkedIn Comment Queue

Manual LinkedIn soft-funnel workflow: find targets, draft comments, decide on follow/connect.

## When to use

- "Who should I comment on?", "next post to comment"
- "Connect request", "should I connect?"
- "Someone liked my post", "LinkedIn targets", "warm signal"
- "Weekly LinkedIn review"

## Important: manual only

This skill helps you READ LinkedIn and DRAFT text. It never posts, likes, follows, or connects automatically. You do the actions yourself in the browser.

## Workflow

### 1. Find the next post to comment on
- Go to your target's recent posts (last 7 days)
- Prioritize posts with engagement (likes/comments) — they're active
- Look for posts where a thoughtful comment adds value, not just "Great post!"

### 2. Draft a comment
- Reference something specific from the post
- Add a perspective, question, or experience
- Keep it 2–4 sentences
- No CTA, no pitch — comment to add value, not sell

### 3. Decide: follow or connect?
- Follow: low commitment, stays in their feed — default action
- Connect: only if you have a reason (shared connection, event, ongoing conversation)
- Check their profile: role, company, recent activity — is there a reason to connect?

### 4. Check engagement on your posts
- Who liked/reacted? Review their profiles
- Anyone worth a comment on their content? A connection?
- Note warm signals: someone who engaged twice is warmer than a one-time liker

## Comment quality bar

- Specific over generic: "The part about X resonated because..." not "Great insights!"
- Adds to the conversation, doesn't just agree
- 2–4 sentences max
- No links in first comment (LinkedIn suppresses them)

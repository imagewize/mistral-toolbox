# Competitor Comparison Pages

Build pages that position your product/service against competitors — for both SEO traffic capture and sales enablement.

## When to use

- "Alternative page", "vs page", "competitor comparison"
- "[Product] vs [Product]", "[Product] alternative"
- "Competitive landing pages", "how do we compare to X"
- "Battle card", "competitor teardown"

## Four formats

### 1. Singular alternative ("X alternative")
Target keyword: "[Competitor] alternative"
- Hero: "Looking for a [Competitor] alternative? Here's why teams switch to [You]"
- Comparison table: feature-by-feature, honest (include where competitor wins)
- Social proof: testimonials from switchers
- CTA: free trial / demo / audit

### 2. Plural alternatives ("X alternatives")
Target keyword: "best [Competitor] alternatives" or "[Category] tools"
- List 5–7 alternatives including yourself
- Brief pros/cons for each
- Comparison table across key criteria
- Position yourself as the top pick with reasoning

### 3. You vs competitor ("X vs Y")
Target keyword: "[You] vs [Competitor]"
- Side-by-side comparison table
- "When to choose [You]" and "When to choose [Competitor]" sections (builds trust)
- Pricing comparison if public
- Migration story: how easy is it to switch

### 4. Competitor vs competitor ("X vs Y" where neither is you)
Target keyword: "[CompetitorA] vs [CompetitorB]"
- Objective comparison
- Subtle positioning: "And here's how [You] compares to both"
- Captures high-intent comparison traffic

## Rules

- Be honest — acknowledge where competitors are strong; it builds credibility
- Use real, verifiable claims only
- Update quarterly — competitor features change
- Include structured data (ComparisonTable or FAQPage)
- Link to your relevant service/product pages

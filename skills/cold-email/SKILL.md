# Cold Email / Outbound Sequences

Write cold emails and follow-up sequences that get replies.

## When to use

- "Cold outreach", "prospecting email", "cold email campaign"
- "Sales email", "SDR email", "write a cold email"
- "Follow-up email sequence", "nobody's replying to my emails"
- "Email to leads", "reach out to prospects"

## Subject line rules

- Under 50 characters
- No spam triggers: "free", "guarantee", "act now", ALL CAPS, multiple !!!
- Personalized when possible: reference their company, role, or a recent event
- Curiosity-driven or benefit-driven, never both

## Opening line

- First line must earn the second line
- Reference something specific about them (not "I hope this finds you well")
- Examples: a post they wrote, a product launch, a shared connection, a mutual pain point

## Body structure (keep under 100 words)

1. Hook: one specific observation about them
2. Relevance: one sentence on why you're reaching out (the bridge between their world and yours)
3. Proof: one concrete result ("We helped [similar company] achieve [result] in [timeframe]")
4. CTA: one low-friction ask ("Worth a 10-minute call next week?" or "Can I send over a relevant case study?")

## Follow-up sequence (3–5 touches over 2–3 weeks)

| Touch | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Initial outreach |
| 2 | Day 3–4 | Follow-up: add value (relevant article, case study, insight) |
| 3 | Day 7–10 | Different angle or channel (LinkedIn connection + note) |
| 4 | Day 14–17 | Breakup email: "I'll stop reaching out — happy to reconnect if priorities change" |

## Personalization at scale

- Segment by role, industry, company size
- Use merge fields for: name, company, specific pain point
- Reference a trigger event: funding round, hiring, new product, leadership change
- Never fake personalization — if you can't find something real, keep it generic but relevant

## Metrics to track

- Open rate (aim 40%+)
- Reply rate (aim 5–10% for cold, 15%+ for warm)
- Positive reply rate (the metric that matters)
- Meeting booked rate
